A production company built around the story.

Shanthi Productions is a film and creative production company. We shape ideas into finished work for the screen, from the first conversation about a script to the final grade and sound mix.

Every project starts with a simple question: what does this story need to feel true on screen? The answer guides our casting, our camera, our edit, and the way we work with the people involved.
